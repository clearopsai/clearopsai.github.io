NC-09 · INTERPRETATION

Administrative change volume and human discussion are different kinds of activity in this tracker: 76.0% of 9,947 changelog events are written by one automated account, while 9.9% of 1,414 comments are automated.

What this rests on.

Changelog share is computed over distinct changelog events and comment share over comment rows. The two denominators are different and are never combined. None of the automated changelog events touches a status, a resolution or an owner.

What argues against it.

Each automated changelog event is the trace of a real pull request action, so the volume is not noise. It measures bookkeeping rather than absence of work.

Holds only if That the author string identifies automation, and that a changelog event and a comment are comparable units of recorded activity. Recorded as partial.

The alternative reading recorded. That they are not comparable at all, in which case the pairing shows only that two different record types have different automation profiles, which is a weaker statement than it looks.

What this rests on.

Derived resolutions: 34 issues end at 'Duplicate'. SELECT DISTINCT key FROM link WHERE link_type='Duplicate' -> 40. Intersection 27, difference 7, and 13 carry a Duplicate link without resolving Duplicate.

34 - 27 = 7 and 40 - 27 = 13, so the four figures are internally consistent.

Link-type inventory: Reference 73, Duplicate 46, Completes 13, Problem/Incident 9, Cloners 6, Supercedes 7, Blocker 5, Required 4, dependent 4, others 5 rows.

What argues against it.

13 of the 40 hold the relation only as 'is duplicated by' - they are the original, not the duplicate - and all 13 resolved Fixed.

3 of the 27 'have both' carry their Duplicate link only in the 'is duplicated by' direction, so the count of issues recorded as duplicating a named other issue is 24, not 27.

Only 3 of the 7 duplicates without a Duplicate link (ZOOKEEPER-3927, 4237, 4239) have no link of any type; the other 4 point at a related or superseding issue by another link type.

Holds only if That carrying a Duplicate link means the issue is a duplicate of something, so that the 40 and the 34 are two measurements of the same thing and the 7 are a linkage gap. Recorded as partial.

The alternative reading recorded. A Duplicate link is a directed relation and the corpus stores both ends. 13 of the 40 hold only the 'is duplicated by' end - they are the original that survived, and every one of them resolved Fixed, which is the correct outcome for an original, not a hygiene defect. Read the 40 as a duplicate population and a third of it is the opposite of a duplicate. The 7 also deserve a second look: 4 of them do carry links of other types towards a related issue (ZOOKEEPER-1677 and ZOOKEEPER-2490 both point at ZOOKEEPER-3726 as 'is fixed by', ZOOKEEPER-3729 references ZOOKEEPER-3730, ZOOKEEPER-2512 depends on ZOOKEEPER-2489), so only 3 issues resolved Duplicate carry no link of any kind.

Holds only if That a comment written after closure, naming a test or a measured result, is evidence the outcome was checked, and that its absence means the outcome was not recorded in this tracker. Recorded as partial.

Whether recurring work families can be re-established is open. The historical arithmetic checks out, but only 9 of 11 family key lists survive in the artifact, covering 113 distinct issues rather than the 141 claimed.

What this rests on.

Ten families excluding the pre-2019 backlog family sum to 153 declared memberships over 141 distinct issues, implying 12 double memberships, and that arithmetic is internally consistent. Nine key lists are recoverable, giving 121 memberships over 113 distinct issues with 8 observed doubles.

What argues against it.

Holds only if That the family groupings described in the historical artifact are reconstructible from what it records. Recorded as external evidence required.

NC-01 · UNKNOWN

This corpus cannot support any statement about how much of what the operation opens it also closes, because every issue in it was selected for having been resolved inside the window.

What this rests on.

The selection rule is statusCategory=Done with a resolution date inside the window, so the corpus contains no open issue by construction and no denominator for an open rate exists in it.
