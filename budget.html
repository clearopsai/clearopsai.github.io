Fixed price. No hourly billing, no change orders, no second invoice. Half on signature, half when the report is delivered, each payable within fourteen days.

What arrives, before the final meeting rather than after it

  1. The estate on one page. Every system, its owner, its annual cost and its renewal date. The page you hand a new hire, or a successor.
  2. The dependency map. What stops working when each thing stops working, written down rather than held in one person's head.
  3. The ranked fix list. Ordered by risk removed per hour spent, so you can stop at the third item if that is the budget.
  4. The board version. One page a trustee or business officer can read and act on without a translation.
  5. The expiry calendar. Every licence, contract and certificate that lapses, dated, with a named person beside each one.

All of it is yours. Forward it, act on it, or hand it to another vendor. There is no restriction on any of it.

Why it is not a sales visit

  • Nothing is implemented. That is exactly why the list is honest.
  • No hardware or software is resold. Nothing recommended pays a commission.
  • No vendor pays a referral fee. Not one, in either direction.
  • There is no phase two. Nothing follows unless you ask for it.

Access is read-only wherever read-only exists. Configuration and contracts only: no student records, no donor data, no employee files. Every credential is revoked on the last day.

The questions a business officer usually asks

  • Does this commit us to anything? No. One document, one price, and it ends.
  • Will it disrupt anything? No. Two scheduled calls, and read-only access.
